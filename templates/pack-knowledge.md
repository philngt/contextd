## Global Principles

- `pack-{your-name}-principle-one` — State a durable constraint, its reason,
  and the evidence needed to check it. Separate MUST rules from advice.
- Distinguish observations, mechanism hypotheses, recommendations, and accepted
  decisions. Missing evidence is a gap, not permission to invent a fact.
- A strategy exception never relaxes an engine or workspace constraint.
- Record official baselines and their review dates; pin changing behavior in
  workspace contracts. Do not claim a fresh review merely for editing prose.
- Treat cognition categories as authoring lenses, not an intelligence ranking
  or a mandatory runtime sequence. Request only the guidance the task needs.
